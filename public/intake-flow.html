Counseling Intake — branching logic

← back to the intake preview

How the wizard decides what to ask. Diamonds are decisions driven by an answer; everyone flows through the common sections (Issue → Family → Health → Spiritual → Money) at the bottom.

flowchart TD
  A["Name · Age · Address · Occupation · Sex"] --> B["Marital status
Single · Dating · Married · Separated · Divorced · Widowed"]
  B -->|Divorced / Widowed| DW["When did it happen? · what happened? ·
how many times married?"]
  DW --> ISSUE
  B -->|Single / Dating / Married / Separated| HB["Have you ever been… (check all)
Single (never married): just “Have you ever been engaged?”
Dating: Engaged · Divorced · Widowed · None
Married: Separated · Divorced · Widowed · None
Separated: Divorced · Widowed · None"]
  HB --> HQ["For EACH box checked: when? · what happened?
Single / Dating: + how many times? (each)
Married + Divorced or Widowed checked: how many times married?
Separated (ALWAYS): times separated · when · what happened · times married"]
  HQ --> S{"Status now?"}
  S -->|Single| ISSUE
  S -->|Dating| P["The person you're dating:
name · willing to come? · in favor of you coming?"]
  S -->|Married| SP["Spouse (original PDI wording):
name · willing to come? · in favor of you coming? ·
years married · how you met · contact · may we contact?"]
  S -->|Separated| SPS["Spouse (same as Married)
+ living together?"]
  P --> ISSUE
  SP --> HX
  SPS --> HX
  HX["Original PDI: spouse previously married? ·
ever filed for divorce?"] --> ISSUE
  ISSUE["WHAT BRINGS YOU HERE
problem · what you've done · how you see yourself & others ·
impact on others · under pressure · fears · what you want ·
Problem Checklist → explain checked boxes"] --> FAM{"Have children?"}
  FAM -->|Yes| KIDS["Children: names/ages/genders ·
relationship with your children"]
  FAM -->|No| PAR
  KIDS --> PAR["Relationship with your parents"]
  PAR --> IL["In-laws
(only if ever married)"]
  IL --> HLTH["HEALTH:
rating · conditions · illnesses/injuries · sleep · physician · meds ·
drugs · alcohol · smoke · upsets ·
seen a counselor? → diagnosis · arrested?"]
  HLTH --> CH{"Attend church?"}
  CH -->|Yes| CHY["Which church · member? · times/month"]
  CH -->|No| SPIR
  CHY --> SPIR["SPIRITUAL:
belief · Christian? · baptized? · pray · Bible ·
recent changes · testimony"]
  SPIR --> MON["MONEY & TIME:
debt → which areas · how much · money a struggle? ·
hrs on games / internet / social"]
  MON --> DONE(["Review → Submit"])

Everyone answers "Have you ever been" right after status. Only people with a current partner (Dating, Married, Separated) get partner/spouse questions.   Questions marked optional elsewhere still show here but aren't required.
